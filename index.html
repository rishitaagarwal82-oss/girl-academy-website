About GIRL Academy

WHY GIRL ACADEMY?

The female body and mind is still constantly treated as a taboo subject. For years, women have been purposely excluded from crucial research, clinical trials, and have been misdiagnosed due to this ignorance. In 2026, many advancements have begun to finally overcome this gender gap in research and treatment, but the path for this change will be very long. We have a lot to catch up on.

This is why GIRL Academy was born. Our organization firmly believes that young people have the drive to make this change happen, which is why we target young minds. At GIRL Academy we also believe that change cannot truly occur if it is not present on a local level. Therefore, our focus is to not only teach and guide young leaders to pioneer in women’s health, we also want to ensure that what they learn is being implemented in their communities.
