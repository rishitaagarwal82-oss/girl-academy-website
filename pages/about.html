About Us

Global innovation, research, and learning for girls and women around the world.

Our Mission

At GIRL Academy, we believe meaningful change begins with understanding the science behind women's health challenges and giving young women the tools to become part of the solution.

Our students learn how to evaluate scientific evidence, explore emerging research, collaborate with mentors, and develop community-centered projects that address real healthcare challenges.

By combining education with leadership, we aim to prepare future researchers, healthcare professionals, and changemakers who will help shape a healthier and more equitable future for women around the world.

The GIRL in GIRL Academy stands for Global Innovation, Research, and Leadership. That is the main focus of our organization: advancing women's health one community at a time, using young minds to accelerate change.

The Academy

The GIRL Academy consists of Fall, Spring, and Summer cohorts, each lasting 14 weeks. Each cohort follows three stages designed to take students from learning to research to action.

01

Learn

Students learn R coding, bibliometrics, and how to read and conduct research. They also explore major women's health issues within their chosen subject.

02

Discover

Students work with a mentor and their track group to study the research landscape surrounding a topic related to their subject. They produce a scientific report of their findings.

03

Act

Students examine how an issue affects their local community through surveys, interviews, and/or observations, then develop an action plan to address it.
