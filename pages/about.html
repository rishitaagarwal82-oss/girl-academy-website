About Us

Global innovation, research, and learning for girls and women around the world.

Our Mission

At GIRL Academy, we believe meaningful change begins with understanding the science behind women's health challenges and giving young women the tools to become part of the solution. Our students learn how to evaluate scientific evidence, explore emerging research, collaborate with mentors, and develop community-centered projects that address real healthcare challenges. By combining education with leadership, we aim to prepare future researchers, healthcare professionals, and changemakers who will help shape a healthier and more equitable future for women around the world. The GIRL in GIRL Academy stands for Global Innovation, Research, and Leadership, and that is the main focus of our organization. Our main goal is to advance women’s health one community at a time, using young minds to speed up the process towards change. We are where passion meets action, and it does not back down.

The Academy

The Academy The GIRL Academy consists of a Fall, Spring, and Summer Cohort, each 14 weeks long. We run the cohorts with the following structure:

Learn

Students will be taught R coding, Bibliometrics, and how to read and conduct research. They will also be taught the main issues on women’s health in general and within their subject.

Discover

Students will work with a mentor in a group within their track to study the research landscape surrounding a topic related to their subject. They will produce a scientific report of their findings.

Act

Students will see how this issue affects their community on a local level. They will conduct surveys, interviews, and/or observations to then support their action plan to imrpove this issue on a local level.
